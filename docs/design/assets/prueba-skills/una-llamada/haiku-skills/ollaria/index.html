Made slowly, on purpose

Three things shape every piece we sell and every seat we book. None of them are rushed.

Fired in batches of 40

We load the kiln once and fire forty pieces together. Each bowl, mug, and plate shares its firing with its neighbors, so you own a piece of a specific batch, not a line in a catalog.

Batches are listed by their firing on each product page.

Four pieces from one shelf, same heat, same night.

Glazes mixed in-house

Every glaze starts as raw minerals weighed and blended at the studio bench. The colors shift a little from batch to batch, which is why no two firings look exactly alike.

Swatches from the studio bench, fired and unfired.

Workshops every Saturday

Come in on a Saturday, sit at the wheel, and take home what you make. Workshops run every week, so you can book the seat that fits your calendar and let the morning be slow.

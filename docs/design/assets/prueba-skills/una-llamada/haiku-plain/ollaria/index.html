How we make it

Slow on purpose, so each piece gets room

We do not make for stock. We make for a batch, we mix our own glazes, and we open the studio to people who want to learn the wheel.

  • Fired in batches of 40

    Every kiln load holds forty pieces. Nothing is rushed through a single firing, and nothing is made to fill a warehouse.

    Small batches, same care

  • Glazes mixed in-house

    We weigh and mix every glaze in our studio. Each tone is tested on real clay before it reaches a shelf, so the color you see is the color you get.

    Mixed by hand, tested on clay

  • Workshops every Saturday

    Pull up a stool and center your first cylinder with a teacher beside you. Workshops run every Saturday, with seats held for you until you arrive.

    A seat at the wheel, every week
